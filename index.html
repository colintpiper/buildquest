01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Why a Retro Game?

🎮
Nostalgia Meets Brand
Late 80s/90s game aesthetics resonate with our contractor audience's generation. A pixel-art browser game is unexpected, memorable, and shareable.
📈
Product Education Through Play
Every power-up maps to a real BuildOps feature. Players learn what OpsAI and our platform do — without sitting through a demo.
🏆
Viral Leaderboard Competition
Contractors are competitive by nature. Public leaderboards and shareable scores drive organic reach across our customer base and beyond.
